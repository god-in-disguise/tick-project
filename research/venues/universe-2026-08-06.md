## TICK Qualification Gates

Every candidate gets the same read-only worksheet:

```text
1. Is the venue live and accessible from our deployment region?
2. What assets and leverage are live now?
3. What is the minimum notional and minimum collateral?
4. What are maker, taker, spread, funding, liquidation, and gas costs?
5. Is execution direct matching, RFQ, pool pricing, keeper callback, or oracle callback?
6. What event proves the fill and the position state?
7. Can a scoped agent or session key trade without withdrawal authority?
8. Can TICK use a shared normalized account model?
9. What is the real p50/p95 request-to-fill latency?
10. Can deposits, closes, liquidations, and withdrawals reconcile deterministically?
```
